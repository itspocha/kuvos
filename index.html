The execution gap

The episode ends at discharge. The plan does not.

A hospital may complete the clinical episode, but the patient still has to execute the plan at home. AHRQ describes discharge as a transfer of information from clinicians to patients and families — medications, warning signs, test results, and follow-up appointments all requiring clear communication and engagement. NCQA's Transitions of Care measure set tracks notification of admission, receipt of discharge information, patient engagement within 30 days, and medication reconciliation after discharge.

The gapThe plan is issued. It is not executed.

The organisation issues the plan, but the patient or supporter must translate it into repeated daily behaviour after leaving the facility.

The riskVisibility is lost, not the plan.

The post-discharge plan loses visibility, comprehension, or follow-through in the home — especially where incapacity affects execution.

How to measure itFall-off, not a borrowed statistic.

Low conversion should be measured as fall-off from discharge instruction to confirmed engagement, completed authorised actions, follow-up connection, or medication reconciliation.

Kuvos is positioned as a care-transition execution layer, not a readmission-prevention guarantee. Sources: AHRQ — Care Transitions From Hospital to Home (IDEAL Discharge Planning); NCQA — Transitions of Care; OECD — Integrated care delivery.

Who Kuvos is for

A trusted protocol for institutions responsible for continuity of care.

Hospitals, insurers, and healthcare-data buyers use Kuvos to extend authorised care plans into the home, support defined high-need populations, and access governed confirmation-event data under explicit permissions. Patients and care circles are the secondary served users whose experience generates the operational value.

Review view

Uncertainty is held, not guessed.

The ingestion layer extracts a constrained field set — task or medication name, dose or instruction, timing, frequency, duration — and preserves a link back to the source page or region for every item. Conflicting or uncertain fields are surfaced to the reviewer rather than silently resolved.

Model agreement is not clinical approval. The Locked Draft State is a product and liability boundary: before authorization, the patient sees no actionable protocol.
